Unified architecture — intent → control → evidence

The portfolio is structured around a single engineering approach composed of three parts:

  • Chinje — intent and conformance: evaluates whether a running system remains aligned with its authorized intent and governs resolution when it drifts.
  • λ-Safe — invariants and safe degradation: formalizes which guarantees must hold and what reduced operating modes are permitted.
  • NiGeNJe Control Fabric — runtime transition control: admits or rejects consequential operations, contains unsafe behaviour, reconciles state and records verifiable evidence.

Key principles

  • Admission before execution
  • Reconciliation, not blind recovery
  • Auditable decisions and evidence

Selected work

λ-Safe — invariant-preserving degradation (Published)

Type-theoretic framework and Rust prototype that makes allowed degradation explicit so systems can continue operating without silently breaking critical invariants.

  • Paper: 10.5281/zenodo.17067644
  • Source: github.com/gee-cpu/lambda-safe

NiGeNJe — runtime control fabric (Specified / in development)

Control platform for admission, containment, constrained optimization, reconciliation and certification of consequential state transitions.

  • Platform specification and architecture documents; focus on policy-as-code admission and decision evidence.

Chinje — intent conformance (Research & reference implementation)

Intent-aware conformance framework that classifies system state as ALIGNED, DRIFTED, UNKNOWN or DEGRADED and governs authorized remediation.
