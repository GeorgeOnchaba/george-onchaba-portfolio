Architecture focus

My work is grounded in one principle: systems should not only stay available, but remain aligned with the intent and constraints they were built to follow.

  • Chinje — intent and conformance: evaluates whether a system remains aligned with its authorized architecture and rules.
  • λ-Safe — invariants and safe degradation: formalizes the guarantees that must still hold when the system is operating under reduced capacity.
  • NiGeNJe — runtime control: governs consequential state transitions, admission, containment, recovery and evidence.

Core principles

  • Intent before execution
  • Evidence before confidence
  • Recovery without violating invariants
  • Traceable decisions
