Published and public

λ-Safe

A type-theoretic framework for invariant-preserving graceful degradation, with a Rust prototype of the compiler. When a component degrades, the type system decides whether the stated invariants still hold.

The open-source Core covers the hard, deterministic guarantees. Probabilistic handling is deliberately kept out of it and belongs to the Enterprise edition.

Drift-oriented computing and the Chinje pattern

My MSc work at Kenyatta University asks how systems should model drift away from a desired state, and how they restore themselves. The Chinje pattern is the software design principle I am formalizing from it.
